Disclosures

How this program works, who provides the legal services, and what we do and do not promise.

Last updated: [Month Day, 2026]

1. Refund Harbor is not a law firm

Refund Harbor ("Refund Harbor," "we," "us," or "our") is a marketing and lead-generation platform. We are not a law firm, accounting firm, or customs broker. We do not practice law, and nothing on this website is, or should be relied upon as, legal, tax, customs, accounting, or financial advice. No content on this site creates an attorney-client relationship between you and Refund Harbor.

2. Who provides the legal services

The tariff refund recovery work described on this site — including eligibility analysis, administrative protests, filings before U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), and any litigation before the U.S. Court of International Trade (CIT) — is performed by specialist trade counsel ("Counsel") engaged separately under a written engagement agreement directly between you and that Counsel or the program operator.

An attorney-client relationship is formed only when you and Counsel execute that written engagement agreement. Submitting an intake form, receiving an eligibility review, or communicating with Refund Harbor does not create an attorney-client relationship. The identity and credentials of the engaged Counsel are provided to you during the eligibility review and before any engagement.

3. Affiliate relationship and compensation

Refund Harbor operates as an affiliate of, or marketing partner to, a third-party tariff refund program (the "Program"). We may receive compensation — including referral fees, a marketing fee, or a share of fees collected — when an importer we refer engages the Program or its Counsel and/or recovers a refund. This compensation does not increase the fees you pay.

4. Fees you may pay

The fee structure described on this site is, in summary:

  • Eligibility review — free, with no obligation.
  • No upfront fee — there is no engagement fee to begin. The Program advances the law firm's costs to prepare and file your claim.
  • Legal processing fees — the advanced legal costs, in an amount provided to you in writing at the time of filing. They are reimbursed from your refund at disbursement, and are earned once your claim is filed — meaning they remain payable even if the claim is denied or no refund is received.
  • Contingency fee — 20% of the amount actually recovered, due within 15 days of your business receiving the refund. Advanced legal processing fees are reimbursed from the refund before the contingency is calculated.
  • Optional cash advance — you may be offered an upfront cash advance against an expected refund through a financing partner. If you elect it, the legal processing fees are netted against the contingency instead of taken from the refund, and the financing partner's own costs may apply.

These amounts are summaries for marketing purposes. The controlling fee terms are those set out in the program services agreement between you and the program operator, together with your separate written engagement with Counsel for the legal work, which you should read in full before signing. 5. No guarantee of results

Refunds are not guaranteed. Whether any amount is recoverable depends on your specific import entries, their liquidation status, applicable filing deadlines, the documentation available, and how pending government appeals and future agency or court decisions resolve. Statistics, timelines, and figures on this site (including the $166B figure, the number of affected importers, processing timelines, and rejection rates) are estimates drawn from public reporting and program experience and may change. Past or general results do not predict the outcome of any individual matter.
