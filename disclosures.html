1. DenseWealth is not a law firm

DenseWealth ("DenseWealth," "we," "us," or "our") is a marketing and lead-generation platform. We are not a law firm, accounting firm, or customs broker. We do not practice law, and nothing on this website is, or should be relied upon as, legal, tax, customs, accounting, or financial advice. No content on this site creates an attorney-client relationship between you and DenseWealth.

2. Who provides the legal services

The tariff refund recovery work described on this site — including eligibility analysis, administrative protests, filings before U.S. Customs and Border Protection (CBP), and any litigation before the U.S. Court of International Trade (CIT) — is performed by specialist trade counsel ("Counsel") engaged separately under a written engagement agreement directly between you and that Counsel or the program operator.

An attorney-client relationship is formed only when you and Counsel execute that written engagement agreement. Submitting an intake form, receiving an eligibility review, or communicating with DenseWealth does not create an attorney-client relationship. The identity and credentials of the engaged Counsel are provided to you during the eligibility review and before any engagement.

We may receive compensation — including referral fees, a marketing fee, or a share of fees collected — when an importer we refer engages the Program or its Counsel and/or recovers a refund.
