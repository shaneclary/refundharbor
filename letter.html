To U.S. importers,

If your business imported goods in 2025, there is a real chance you are owed money. After the Supreme Court’s February ruling on IEEPA tariffs, roughly $166 billion is in motion — and most of the 301,000 importers entitled to a piece of it don’t yet know they are. I started Refund Harbor to fix the second half of that problem.

Our mission is simple: get importers the money they are owed, on a timeline that works for them.

The current refund landscape isn’t built for the businesses inside it. Entries processed through the CAPE portal (Phase 1) can be returned in 60–90 days. Historically liquidated entries (Phase 2) can take 12–18 months. Matters that require litigation before the U.S. Court of International Trade can take longer still. For an importer running payroll and ordering next quarter’s containers, “wait a year and a half” is not always an answer.

So we built a program with two timelines in mind. We will pursue your refund the patient way — through proper administrative protests under 19 U.S.C. § 1514, with attention to every documentation requirement that CBP demands. And, where it makes sense for your business, we will work with our financing partner to offer an optional upfront advance against the anticipated refund, so the money is in your hands now rather than later. Either path. Your call.

A few things we will not do. We will not promise a refund — some claims are denied, and some recoveries are smaller than estimated. We will not pretend to be your lawyer; the legal work is done by qualified specialist trade counsel, engaged separately, with the attorney-client relationship and privilege sitting between you and them. And we will not collect a contingency on money you do not receive.

What we will do is the work. Eligibility review, free. Legal filing, advanced and coordinated. Contingency, only on what you actually recover. And a real human on the other end of the line for every step.

If your business paid IEEPA tariffs in 2025, I would like to hear from you. The protest deadline is 180 days from each entry’s liquidation, and those days are running.

Shane Clary
Shane Clary
