The two-variant system, in plain language

Each variant is designed for the background it shows up on. Putting either one on the wrong background fails — that’s by design, not a bug.

  • refundharbor.png — cream/light elements on a transparent ground. Lives on the dark navy header, footer, and disclosure bar. Bare, no stamp pill.
  • refundharbor-alt.svg — navy elements on a transparent ground. Lives on the cream paper of the letter page, and on any future light/cream/white section. The "Mariner's Mark" composition: outer ring, horizon line, four-point gold north star, three gold bathymetric depth arcs, serif wordmark, hairline gold rule, tagline with gold stars.

Production routing

  • index.html, disclosures.html, privacy.html, terms.html, insights/* — header and footer use the PNG (dark navy).
  • letter.html — header/footer use the PNG; the letter-paper head uses the SVG (cream paper).
  • favicon — favicon.svg (the small compass mark) stays. PNGs lose crispness at 16/32 px.

If a future section needs the other variant

Adding a navy section on the homepage where the dark logo wouldn’t read? Use the PNG. Adding a white-background panel where the cream logo wouldn’t read? Use the SVG. The pattern is: pick the variant designed for the background, not the one you used last time.
